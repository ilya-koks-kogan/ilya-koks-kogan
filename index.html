Hello boys and girls!
My name is Ilya, but friends call me ILUHA KOKS. I am a designer from Vladivostok with focus on people, culture and ethics.

Cozmic Artifactz

This is my exploration of how to design interaction and display information within a webpage layout. I like snowboarding, and cosmic occultism is what inspired this work. This was my first experience with interactive prototyping in Figma.

Redeye Records

In this work, I focused on designing user flows and organising interface elements within a mobile application. Initially I created a payment form screen as part of the Daily UI challenge and then it sparked the idea to design a vinyl record store.

iCan Bank

This project is my attempt to revamp user flows and interface elements for an Australian mobile banking app. Additionally, I explored how to work with external components from an existing design system and how to build UI component library.

Lutic AI

The cloud-based platform that aggregates AI tools for generating images, videos and other content. My job involved the entire process from shaping the product vision and defining the visual language, to developing design system and interface solutions.

Detego

The developer of logistics and inventory management software for clients such as Adidas, Levi’s, Bestseller, On and Allbirds. As the sole designer, I led the development of complex interfaces and a comprehensive design system for web, mobile, and desktop applications.

Liquid Mining

The platform that allows users to lease crypto mining equipment and invest in crypto currencies. I worked in a team of 4 designers where we built the entire platform with complex user scenarios, created vast design system along with adaptive layouts.

Coursalytics

The online service for corporate education and training programs designed for executive staff. I teamed up with a designer colleague to revamp the visual aspects of the platform and create web and mobile versions of the service.

OH&S Interface

The occupational health and safety data visualization project for a major mining company. I partnered with a design agency to gather requirements, research the existing processes, and create a holistic dashboard for the OH&S department.

Hormon App

The test assignment for a major green bank where I designed a futuristic super app that allows users to make transactions with their own hormones. This work was about how far I could take my imagination, design thinking and UI skills.
